## How to use this log

**Pre-filled by Claude.** Rows marked `yes?` or `no?` were filled in from the fan wiki's page for this film and from Claude's general knowledge. **None of it has been checked against the film.** While you watch:

- Change `yes?` to `yes` if you see it, or `no` if you don't. Change `no?` to `yes` if a dragon turns up after all.
- Raise **Confidence** to `high` for anything you've seen yourself, and fix the **Appearance** if the guess was wrong.
- Set **Source** to `film` once you've confirmed it on screen.

Then:

1. **Pass 1:** watch the film and add a row to the **Scene log** every time a dragon is seen or named. Timestamps are left empty on purpose; only a viewer can fill them.
2. **Pass 2:** rewatch busy scenes (battles, nests, crowds, books, carvings, maps) for background and pictured-only dragons.
3. Update the **Checklist** from your log. Each dragon gets its single strongest appearance type for this film.
4. Anything you can't identify goes in **Unidentified**, with a description, so it can be checked later.

**Appearance types** (Plan.md §2.3), strongest first:

| Type | Use when |
|---|---|
| `featured` | A named dragon with a role in the story |
| `on_screen` | Clearly seen and identifiable |
| `background` | Visible but incidental; only identifiable on close inspection |
| `mentioned` | Named in dialogue but not seen |
| `pictured` | Seen only as a drawing, carving, book page or object |

When the wiki says "mentioned and pictured", the log uses `mentioned` (the stronger type) and keeps the detail in **Notes**.

**Confidence:** `high` (sure), `medium` (fairly sure), `low` (a guess; recheck).

**Source:** `film` (you saw it), `wiki` (fan wiki page below), `knowledge` (Claude's general knowledge).

Write descriptions in your own words. Don't paste text from wikis or official sites (Plan.md §16).
